Serotine simple mode

Mockups H1 to T1 for the plan in docs/simple-ui.md (drawn 2026-10-26, approved by the owner 2026-10-27), which wins where the two differ. Simple mode is the default view; Settings → Advanced → Detailed view brings back today's screens (docs/wallet-ux.md). Blockies, the QR code, amounts, names and addresses are made up. Opens in a browser with declarative shadow DOM (Chrome 111, Safari 16.4, Firefox 123 or later).

Home and account

Freshness. No block numbers anywhere. Ethereum and Gnosis (no BALs yet) are read when a screen opens and on pull-to-refresh, and say "Updated 2 min ago". Sepolia (BALs) updates live. A balance older than ~1 h is dimmed and left out of the total.

Send

Send. Amount first, fee level folded into one row (Normal by default). The review is a sheet: recipient in full, the most the fee can be, the balance after, and the on-chain check said in words. Blocks, gas and the hash sit under Details.

Swap

Swap. One card. When the allowance falls short the compose screen already says "2 steps". The review numbers them, each with its own fee and its own confirmation. Between them the app waits for step 1, re-quotes and asks again. "Later" leaves step 2 waiting in Activity.

Settings

Detailed view is off by default, in both builds. On, it brings back today's screens: the light-client strip on every screen, blocks and proofs up front, and the Debug tab's switch under Detailed view.
